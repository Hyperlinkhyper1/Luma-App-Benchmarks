How the signalling works

Each coloured track section is a block. A block can contain only one train; every vehicle in a train occupies the blocks under it. Signals show RED when the next block is occupied, no route is set, or the signal is manually held; YELLOW when the next block is clear but the signal after it is red; and GREEN when at least two blocks are clear. Before a train can enter the single-track tunnel/bridge section, an interlocking route is requested: the facing points move, the whole section is locked in one direction, and only then may the signal clear. Conflicting routes cannot be reserved together. Reservations and switches release behind a train as it clears them, so trains wait in the passing loops instead of deadlocking.
