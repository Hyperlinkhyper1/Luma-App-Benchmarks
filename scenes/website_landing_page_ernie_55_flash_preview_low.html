Before your first sip

Questions from
the coffee counter.

Still curious? These answers cover the basics without pretending to be the entire rulebook.

Which roast should I choose?

Morning Light is a medium roast with chocolate and caramel notes. Daybreak is a light roast with citrus and honey. Nightcap Decaf is a decaf medium roast with cocoa and hazelnut. The decaf still contains some caffeine.

Which grind options can I choose?

Whole bean, filter grind, or espresso grind. Whole bean is best if you grind at home; filter suits drippers and batch brewers; espresso is made for a pressurised machine.

Can I pause or change a delivery?

Yes. In the fictional Ember & Bean service, customers can pause or change their next delivery at any time. You can also change the roast, grind, or delivery frequency.

How does this demo work?

This delivered benchmark is an offline demo and does not connect to the fictional Ember & Bean shop. It does not place a real order, take payment, collect an address, or send information anywhere. The confirmation is shown locally in your browser.
