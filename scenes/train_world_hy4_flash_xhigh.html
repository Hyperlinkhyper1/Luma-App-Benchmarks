How the signalling works

Every block of track may contain only one train. A train occupies every block touched by any car. A signal is red when its block is occupied, a route is not set, or you hold it; yellow when the next signal is red; green when at least two blocks are clear.

Before a signal guarding switches clears, a route is reserved through the turnouts. The blades move and stay locked until the train has passed. Conflicting routes cannot be reserved together. The single-track sections are protected by these reservations, and trains wait in station/passing loops. Manual holds are released by clicking the same signal again.
