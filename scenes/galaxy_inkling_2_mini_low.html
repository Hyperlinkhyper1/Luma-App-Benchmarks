How it works

Scale: the ship, systems and galaxy share one world space (~160 units ≈ 50,000 ly). Scale is handled with a logarithmic depth buffer and a generous near/far split, so a 14-unit hull and a 160-unit galaxy coexist without z-fighting. The ship never flies to the galaxy centre; instead the journey route is chosen in world space.

Galaxy: ~225k GPU points with per-star temperature colour, differential rotation in the vertex shader, dark dust-lane point sprites, pink H-II regions, a supermassive black hole with a shader accretion disc and a screen-space gravitational-lens shader.

Route: a Catmull-Rom curve through departure → cruise → hyperspace → galactic core → nebula → arrival.
