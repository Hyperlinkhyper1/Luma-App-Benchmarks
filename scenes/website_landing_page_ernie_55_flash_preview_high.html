How it works

Three steps, then the kettle

No quizzes, no accounts to babysit. Pick a character, tell us your brewer, choose the pace — and keep the ritual you already love.

  1. 1

    Pick your roast

    Chocolate and caramel for a soft start, citrus and honey for a brighter one, or cocoa and hazelnut for the evening. Each is a 250 g bag, freshly roasted.

  2. 2

    Choose your grind

    Keep the beans whole and grind at home, take a filter grind for a pour-over or batch brewer, or take a fine espresso grind for your machine.

  3. 3

    Set your rhythm

    Every two weeks or monthly — both billed per delivery, never as one big yearly lump. Pause, change or skip from your account whenever you need to.

First delivery is roasted the week it ships, then packed the same morning it leaves the roastery. That is the whole trick: short shelf life, short trip.

Start with Morning Light, our medium roast with chocolate and caramel notes. It is forgiving in almost any brewer, tastes sweet with milk, and still has enough body for a black cup.

If you already love a bright filter coffee, go straight to Daybreak, the light roast with citrus and honey. If you want something gentle for the evening, Nightcap Decaf brings cocoa and hazelnut — it is decaffeinated, but it still contains a small amount of caffeine.

Yes. In the coffee selection dialog you can choose whole bean, filter grind or espresso grind. Whole bean keeps the beans freshest — grind just before you brew. Filter grind suits pour-over and batch brewers; espresso grind is finer for a machine.
