Our coffee

Three roasts for three kinds of morning

Light and bright, mellow and comforting, or a gentle decaf for late pours. Each 250 g bag is stamped with its roast date and ground for your brewer.

Morning Light

Medium roastChocolateCaramel

Mellow, round, and endlessly drinkable — like warm chocolate folded into caramel, with a soft sweetness that lingers after the cup. Our everyday default for drip, moka pot, and French press.

$14 / 250 g bag

Daybreak

Light roastCitrusHoney

Bright and lively with a citrus lift and a thread of honey sweetness. Light-bodied and tea-like, it shines in a pour-over or an AeroPress where the delicate aromatics get room to open up.

$14 / 250 g bag

Nightcap Decaf

Decaf medium roastCocoaHazelnut

A decaf that still tastes like coffee: cocoa depth, toasted hazelnut, and a rounded finish. It is decaffeinated, so a trace of caffeine remains, but it pours beautifully for an evening cup without the buzz.

$14 / 250 g bag

Roasted with care

Every batch is roasted in small drum batches, rested for a few days, and sealed with its roast date on the back. Nothing sits in a warehouse losing its aroma.

How it works

From our roaster to your first pour

Three small decisions and a rhythm. That is all it takes to keep good coffee on your counter.

Step 01

Pick your roast

Start with the flavour you are chasing: chocolate and caramel, citrus and honey, or cocoa and hazelnut in a decaf.

Step 02

Choose your grind

Whole bean for your grinder, filter grind for drip and pour-over, or espresso grind for a fine, even extraction.

Step 03

Set your rhythm

Every two weeks or monthly, one bag or four. Change, pause, or skip your next delivery any time before it roasts.

FAQ

Brew questions, answered plainly

Choose by the flavour you want in the cup. Morning Light is a medium roast with chocolate and caramel notes — the safe everyday choice. Daybreak is a light roast with citrus and honey notes, brighter and more delicate. Nightcap Decaf is a decaf medium roast with cocoa and hazelnut notes. All three are 250 g bags.

Three choices at checkout: Whole bean if you grind at home, Filter grind for drip machines and pour-over cones, and Espresso grind for a fine, even extraction. Tell us your brewer and we match the grind to it — grind size matters more than most people expect.

Yes. In this fictional service you can skip, pause, or change the date of your next delivery before it goes to roast, and you can swap your roast or grind any time. Your price per delivery stays the same: $14, $26, or $48 depending on the size, whether you choose every two weeks or monthly.
