Our coffee

Three roasts, three moods

Every bag is roasted for a ritual, not a shop. Pick the lightness you love, then let us handle the rest.

Light roast

Daybreak

Citrus · Honey

Bright and honeyed. Daybreak opens with a sharp citrus peel and lands soft on wildflower honey — made for the sunrise pour.

Medium roast

Morning Light

Chocolate · Caramel

The one on repeat. Dark chocolate over a caramel hum, a warm body, and a finish that never feels heavy — the everyday ritual made perfect.

Medium roast · Decaf

Nightcap Decaf

Cocoa · Hazelnut

A medium decaf with serious depth — cocoa and hazelnut without the jitters. Decaffeinated, not caffeine-free: just gentler, for slower evenings.

Roasted with care

Every batch is dialed to a clear roast profile — from early light to deeper medium — so the cup arrives bright, balanced, and honest, moments after it leaves the roaster.

Made for your brew

Whole bean, filter grind, or espresso grind — choose the texture that matches your dripper, filter, or portafilter, and never settle for a compromise.

Fresh coffee, on your schedule

Recurring deliveries keep the bag full without the thought. Choose your own rhythm — every 2 weeks or monthly — and the coffee arrives when your ritual calls for it.

How it works

No accounts, no hunting. Three decisions get you a bag of coffee that matches how you start the day.

  1. 1

  3. 3
