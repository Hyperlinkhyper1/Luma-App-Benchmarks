HOW IT WORKS

SCALE & PRECISION: To seamlessly bridge the 1:1014 scale gap between a 30m ship and a 100,000 ly galaxy without z-fighting or floating-point jitter, we use a cinematic scale-compression technique. The galaxy is rendered at a visually compressed radius (1500 units) while local objects are scaled up. Depth is stabilized via a logarithmic depth buffer.

GALAXY: 200,000 GPU points are generated in logarithmic spiral arms, a central bar, and a bulge. A custom vertex shader applies differential rotation (inner stars orbit faster), dust lane dimming based on density wave theory, and H-II region emission.

HYPERDRIVE: Warp distorts space by pushing galaxy points along the ship's normalized direction vector via shader uniforms, while the camera FOV widens and engine plumes extend.
