How the signalling works

Blocks. The network is split into one-train-per-block sections. Every block holds any number of cars of one train, never two.

Signals. Each block entry has a 3-aspect signal: red = block occupied/not routed/manual hold, yellow = next block uncertain, green = clear at least two blocks ahead. Trains brake to red, slow for yellow.

Routes. Before a signal at a switch clears, the train reserves the needed blocks and switch. Switches animate and lock while reserved. Opposite trains cannot reserve the same single-track blocks at once.

Single track. The north 2-block section is direction-locked. Competing trains wait on the passing loop until the direction-locked section is clear. Click a signal to force it red; click again to release.
