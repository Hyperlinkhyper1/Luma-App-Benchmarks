Freshly ground answers

FAQ

A few helpful notes before you choose your bag, grind, and morning rhythm.

How do I choose a roast?

Start with the taste you want in the cup. Morning Light is a medium roast with chocolate and caramel; Daybreak is a light roast with citrus and honey; Nightcap Decaf is a decaf medium roast with cocoa and hazelnut. The featured selector updates the illustrated bag and tasting notes.

What grind options are available?

The dialog offers whole bean, filter grind, and espresso grind. Whole bean is for grinding at home. Filter grind suits paper-filter brewing, and espresso grind is for espresso machines.

Can I pause or change a delivery?

Yes—in the fictional Ember & Bean service, you can pause or change your next delivery before it leaves the roastery. This offline demo does not connect to a real service.

How does this demo work?

This delivered benchmark is an offline demo and does not connect to the fictional Ember & Bean shop. The selection dialog shows a local confirmation only. No order is placed, no payment is taken, and no information is sent.
