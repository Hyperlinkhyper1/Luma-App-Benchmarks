FOLD 04 — FIELD NOTES

Designer's signature: every handle scale is laser-cut from a single titanium sheet and folded into a six-sided paper-crane wing. The seams catch the neon lines; the wing's trailing edge forms the grip rings.

Latch: the latch is a neodymium bridge. It floats on a pivoted yoke and meets the handles with a soft magnetic pull — no spring, no button.

Pivot: light titanium yoke, twin 6-ball ceramic bearings, sapphire thrust washers.

Stage: a mossy basalt outcrop in a fern garden. Ferns, spores and dappled light are generated in code. Use Stage view to see it, HERO to return.
