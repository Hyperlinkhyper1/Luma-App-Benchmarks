FAQ

Questions before the kettle sings?

Here is the short, clear version of how this coffee selection works.

“There is no wrong first roast—only the one that makes you want tomorrow’s cup.”

How do I choose a roast?
Start with the tasting notes. Morning Light is a medium roast with chocolate and caramel; Daybreak is a light roast with citrus and honey; Nightcap Decaf is a decaf medium roast with cocoa and hazelnut. You can change your featured roast in the hero before opening the coffee dialog.
What grind options can I choose?
Choose whole bean, filter grind, or espresso grind in the coffee selection dialog. Whole bean keeps grinding in your hands; the other two options match common home brewing routines.
Can I pause or change a delivery?
Yes, within the fictional Ember & Bean service you can pause or change your next delivery before it is prepared. This page does not connect to a real service.
How does this demo work?
This delivered benchmark is an offline demo and does not connect to the fictional Ember & Bean shop. All choices stay on this page; confirming shows a local summary only.
