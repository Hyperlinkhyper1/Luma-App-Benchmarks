How it works:
Scale is managed via a Floating Origin system. The ship remains strictly at the scene origin (0,0,0) while the universe dynamically translates around it, preventing floating-point precision loss (jitter). A logarithmic depth buffer seamlessly handles depth ranges from meters to light-years. Procedural geometry, custom shaders, and canvas noise generate all visuals with zero external assets.
