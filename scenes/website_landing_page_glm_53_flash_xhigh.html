Roasted with care

Every batch is roasted to order in our little drum roaster, logged by hand, and rested for two days before bagging — so the gases that make a cup taste baked have somewhere to go besides your brew. When the flame settles, we cup the batch before a single bag is sealed.

How it works

Three small decisions, then the ritual runs itself.

  1. Step 01

    Pick your roast

    Three coffees, three temperaments. Choose the one that sounds like your morning — or let the featured roast choose you.

  2. Step 02

    Choose your grind

    Whole bean, filter, or espresso. If you grind at home, we send whole beans and stay out of your way.

  3. Step 03

    Set your rhythm

    Every two weeks or monthly. Your bag ships freshly roasted; pause or move the date anytime.
