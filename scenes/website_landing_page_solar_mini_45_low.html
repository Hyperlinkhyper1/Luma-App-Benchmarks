Questions, poured slow

Everything worth asking before the first cup

Which roast should I choose?

Start with Morning Light if you like chocolate and caramel, Daybreak if you want citrus and honey, or Nightcap Decaf for cocoa and hazelnut without a stimulant boost. Decaf still contains a small amount of caffeine.

Can I choose how the beans are ground?

Yes. Choose whole bean to brew at home, filter grind for drip machines and pour-over, or espresso grind for a pressurized machine. We grind for your brew rather than shipping all of it whole.

Can I pause or change my next delivery?

In the real Ember & Bean service, you could pause or change the next delivery before it shipped. In this offline demo, the controls are display only — no deliveries are scheduled or changed.

How does this demo work?

This is an offline design benchmark of a fictional coffee shop. It runs from a single local HTML file with no network, account or backend. Choosing a coffee opens a form that validates your name and email, shows a live order summary and, on submit, confirms the selection locally.

Does my information go anywhere?

No. Nothing is sent, stored or charged. The name and email stay in the page's memory, disappear on refresh, and the confirmation states clearly that the demo did not place an order.
