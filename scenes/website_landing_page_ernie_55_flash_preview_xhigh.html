Answers before you pour

Questions from the brewing bench.

If something here still leaves you guessing, the demo below lets you try the whole flow without a real order.

Start with Morning Light, our medium roast with chocolate and caramel notes. It is forgiving across brewers and pleasantly sweet. If you prefer a brighter cup, pick Daybreak, a light roast with citrus and honey notes. For evenings, Nightcap Decaf is a decaf medium roast with cocoa and hazelnut notes — it still contains a little caffeine, just far less than a regular roast.

Choose whole bean, filter grind, or espresso grind. Whole bean keeps the roast at its most flexible if you own a grinder. Filter grind is set for pour-over cones and drip machines, and espresso grind is finer for an espresso machine. The grind choice is carried into your live order summary and the demo confirmation.

Yes. In the fictional Ember & Bean service you can pause, change your next delivery, or switch between the every two week and monthly schedules. Prices stay the same either way — $14, $26, and $48 per delivery — and billing always follows the selected schedule.
