Before you brew

A few useful notes.

Morning Light is a medium roast with chocolate and caramel, suited to a softer everyday cup. Daybreak is light, with citrus and honey, for a brighter pour-over. Nightcap Decaf is a decaffeinated medium roast with cocoa and hazelnut; it is not caffeine-free.
Choose whole bean if you like to grind at home. Filter grind suits pour-over and drip methods. Espresso grind is finer and made for short, intense espresso-based cups.
