FAQ

Good questions, before the kettle boils.

Here is what to know about roasts, grinds, and how this coffee demo behaves.

How do I choose the right roast?

Start with the flavors you like. Morning Light is a medium roast with chocolate and caramel notes. Daybreak is a light roast with citrus and honey notes. Nightcap Decaf is a decaf medium roast with cocoa and hazelnut notes.

Which grind options can I pick?

Choose whole bean, filter grind, or espresso grind. Whole bean is ideal if you grind just before brewing; filter suits drip, pour-over, and press; espresso grind is made for a portafilter.

Can I pause or change a delivery?

In the fictional Ember & Bean service, you can pause or change your next delivery before it is prepared. You can move between suitable frequencies and adjust the next coffee choice.

How does this demo work?

This delivered benchmark is an offline demo and does not connect to the fictional Ember & Bean shop. The form checks your entries in the browser only; it sends no information, takes no payment, and places no real order.
